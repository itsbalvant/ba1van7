About

Who is 0x0doteth?

0x0doteth is the security research handle of Balvant Chavda (also written as Chavda Balvant). Balvant is an independent cybersecurity researcher from India, specializing in vulnerability discovery, bug bounty hunting, and responsible disclosure.

Under the handle 0x0doteth, Balvant has published 11+ CVEs affecting products from Adobe Commerce, Adobe Content Credentials, and Nextcloud. He has been acknowledged in security Hall of Fame programs by Google, Microsoft, Apple, Meta, Amazon Web Services, Adobe, GitHub, Intel, Coinbase, PayPal, Salesforce, and over a dozen other organizations.

The username 0x0doteth appears on HackerOne, Adobe Security Bulletins, Nextcloud Security Advisories, and GitHub security advisories as a credited researcher.
